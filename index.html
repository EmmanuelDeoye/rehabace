About Rehabace

Rehabace is a specialized therapy design and equipment company dedicated to creating environments that support healing, development, and independence. We focus on designing and building functional therapy spaces for clinics, schools, homes, and rehabilitation centers.

Our approach combines practical therapy knowledge with thoughtful interior design. By working closely with occupational therapists, physiotherapists, educators, and healthcare professionals, we create spaces that are not only visually appealing but also clinically effective for therapy and rehabilitation.

Whether it is a sensory room for children with developmental needs, a rehabilitation area for physical therapy, or a calming space for emotional regulation, our goal is simple — to build environments that make therapy more engaging, comfortable, and effective.

  • ✨ Sensory room design, construction, and equipment installation
  • 🛋️ Functional therapy furniture and rehabilitation layouts
  • 🤝 Collaboration with therapists, schools, and healthcare centers
